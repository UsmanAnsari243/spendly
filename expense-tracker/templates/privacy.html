Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly, we collect only what is needed to run your account and your expense tracking:

  • Account details, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense entries you record, including amount, category, date and description.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, needed to keep the service working.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the service: to show your expenses, build your category breakdowns and summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and kept for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is completely secure. You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please get in touch with the Spendly team using the contact details provided on the site.
